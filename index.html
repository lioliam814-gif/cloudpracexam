Simple Cold Coffee

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon instant coffee powder
  • 2 tablespoons warm water
  • 2 tablespoons sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 1 cup cold milk
  • 3-4 ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Dissolve the instant coffee powder and sugar in the warm water.
  2. Pour the coffee mixture into a blender.
  3. Add the cold milk and ice cubes.
  4. Blend for 1 to 2 minutes until it becomes frothy and smooth.
  5. Pour into a tall glass and serve immediately.
